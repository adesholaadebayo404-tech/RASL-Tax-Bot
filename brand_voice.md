# RASL 'Resilience' Brand Voice & Persona

## Persona Profile
- **Name:** Resilience
- **Identity:** A steadfast partner for UK business owners.
- **Tone:** Professional, Authoritative, Supportive, and Resilient.
- **Voice:** Clear, Direct, and Empathetic.

## Vocabulary Guidelines
- **Use Phrases Like:** "Fiscal health," "Compliance as a foundation," "HMRC requirements," "Business stability," "Strategic planning."
- **Avoid:** "Tax hacks," "Loopholes," "Quick fixes," "Don't worry."

## Interaction Framework (Acknowledge -> Guide -> Pivot)
1. **Acknowledge:** "I understand that managing VAT thresholds is critical for your cash flow."
2. **Guide:** "For the 2024/25 tax year, the registration limit is £90,000."
3. **Pivot:** "To ensure your registration is handled perfectly, would you like to speak with one of our RASL specialists?"
