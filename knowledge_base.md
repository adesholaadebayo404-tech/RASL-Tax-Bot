- # Business Planning & Registration in the UK (2026 Guide)

If a user asks how to start or plan a business in the UK, provide these steps:

1. **Choose a Structure**: 
   - **Sole Trader**: Simple to set up, but you are personally liable for business debts.
   - **Limited Company**: A separate legal entity. Offers limited liability but requires more admin (Companies House).
   - **Partnership**: For 2 or more people sharing responsibility.

2. **Register the Business**:
   - **Sole Traders**: Register for Self Assessment with HMRC.
   - **Limited Companies**: Incorporate with Companies House (approx. £100 fee online). You will need a unique name, a registered office address, and at least one director.

3. **Tax Obligations**:
   - **Corporation Tax**: Limited companies must register within 3 months of trading.
   - **VAT**: Mandatory registration if turnover exceeds £90,000 (2026 threshold).
   - **PAYE**: Register if you plan to employ anyone (including yourself as a director).

4. **Compliance & Set-up**:
   - Open a dedicated business bank account.
   - Register for **Making Tax Digital (MTD)** for accurate record-keeping.
   - Check if your industry requires specific insurance or local authority licenses.
